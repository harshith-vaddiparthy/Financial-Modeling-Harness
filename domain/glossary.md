# Vocabulary

MRR: recurring revenue for one modeled month. ARR: MRR × 12 at a point in time, not annual recognized revenue. GMV: marketplace transaction value before the take rate. COGS: direct cost of revenue. CAC: modeled sales/marketing spend per new customer. NRR: retained cohort revenue after churn/expansion, excluding new customers.

DSO/DIO/DPO: days of receivables, inventory and payables. PP&E: net property, plant and equipment. CFO/CFI/CFF: cash from operations, investing and financing. Net income: accrual earnings after interest and tax. Burn: positive operating/investing cash consumption before financing. Funding gap: projected cash below zero. Priced-round dilution: new cash investment divided by post-money valuation.
