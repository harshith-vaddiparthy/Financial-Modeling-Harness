# Domain brief

The founder supplies the business, dates, currency, opening balances and operating plans. The agent maps the evidence into a structured input, asks only for material missing information and explains proposed assumptions. The calculation engine owns arithmetic; the agent owns interpretation and traceable recommendations.

The startup skill informs cohort revenue, headcount, unit economics, runway and funding scenarios. The integrated-financial-modeling skill informs statement linkages, working capital, debt and retained earnings. Both are preserved as pinned references. The deterministic runtime corrects inconsistent upstream example figures and calculates cash from cash flow rather than balancing the statement with a cash plug.
